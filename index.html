The Strike

On a night when Kurukkanmoola was bathed in the light of an ancient comet, a rare planetary alignment triggered a lightning bolt of unimaginable power.

It didn't just strike once. It struck two men. Jaison, a simple tailor with big dreams, and Shibu, a lonely soul searching for purpose. One became a saviour; the other, a shadow.

Directed by Basil Joseph, this cinematic masterpiece redefined the superhero genre for Indian cinema, blending local heart with global scale.
