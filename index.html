THE STRIKE

Kurukkanmoola, 1994. A planetary alignment that happens once in 700 years.

A rare bolt of lightning strikes Jaison and Shibu simultaneously. One finds strength in his goodness, the other finds justification for his pain.

Basil Joseph crafts a superhero epic that remains deeply rooted in the heart of Kerala.
