THE ORIGIN

In 1994, a rare planetary alignment triggers a massive lightning strike in the small village of Kurukkanmoola. Jaison, a tailor, and Shibu, an outcast, are both struck by the same bolt.

Gaining extraordinary speed and reflexes, Jaison must decide whether to use his powers for personal gain or protect his village from a dark force rising from the same lightning.
