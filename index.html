THE STRIKE

Kurukkanmoola, 1994. A planetary alignment that happens once in 700 years.

A rare bolt of lightning, carrying the energy of a thousand storms, strikes the village. Two men are caught in its path. One discovers the hero within, the other is consumed by the power.

This is not just a superhero movie; it's the heart of Kerala, brought to life with a cinematic scale never seen before in Malayalam cinema.
